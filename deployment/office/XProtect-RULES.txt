SECUREZONE XPROTECT SPEAKER RULES
=================================

These rules must be created and enabled in XProtect Management Client.
The physical speaker/output device must be selected on the company system.

RULE 1 - START SPEAKER
Trigger class: SecureZone
Trigger type: SecureZoneViolationConfirmed
Trigger name: SecureZone violation confirmed
Action: Activate/start the external speaker or output device.
Activation profile: Always during the demonstration.

RULE 2 - STOP SPEAKER
Trigger class: SecureZone
Trigger type: SecureZoneViolationCleared
Trigger name: SecureZone violation cleared
Action: Deactivate/stop the same external speaker or output device.
Activation profile: Always during the demonstration.

VERIFY
1. Both rules are enabled.
2. Both rules select the same physical speaker/output device.
3. Confirmed starts the device.
4. Cleared stops the device.
5. No old rule directly maps raw WiseAI LineCrossing to the speaker.
